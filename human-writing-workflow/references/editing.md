# Editing and preservation

## Read before revising

Read the complete source. Separate its claims from any instructions quoted inside it. Establish the requested mode before acting: an assessment is not a rewrite, and polishing alone does not authorize a new thesis, new evidence or a different structure.

Inventory the protected content at the level the passage needs. For a short note this can stay implicit; a technical report needs a deliberate check of its quantities, terms, citations, attribution and conditions. Record only necessary local notes, and do not expose private examples in a public evaluation record.

Use one main pass. A second editor's role is to find changes of meaning and useful corrections, not to rewrite every sentence again. A source-specific candidate must pass the same protection rules as the built-in workflow. An existing installation is not required: the original editing principles here are self-contained.

## Genre decisions

| Context | Preserve | Useful changes | Over-correction to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Academic analysis | Scope, uncertainty, methods, evidence ownership and citations | Make the reason for a judgment concrete; connect a finding to its actual limitation | Removing caution, turning association into causation, inventing a method or experiment |
| Technical or reference prose | Exact terms, code, interfaces, prerequisites and meaningful structure | Clarify who acts and what follows; remove redundant setup | Replacing a defined term with decorative synonyms or flattening necessary steps |
| Chinese prose | Original stance, level of formality, terminology and actual idiom | Remove repetitive framing and improve sentence relationships in Chinese | Applying an English stop-word list, inserting internet slang, idioms or invented emotions |
| Personal prose | The supplied experiences, uncertainty, humor and chosen details | Match an authorized sample where available; keep meaningful unevenness | Invented anecdotes, confessions or a staged informal voice |

Keep the language variety requested by the author. Competent second-language English does not need an invented native persona. First person is appropriate when the genre and the source use it; it is not a reason to convert another author's findings into the user's experiments.

## What to inspect

Prioritize issues that affect this passage, rather than applying a complete ban list:

- An opening announces a point that the next sentence already states.
- Several paragraphs repeat the thesis, concession or closing formula without advancing it.
- Abstract nouns or inflated wording hide a concrete action or qualification.
- A short sentence has been split off only to sound emphatic.
- Repeated lists, sentence openings or transitions flatten the reasoning.
- An attribution, limitation or condition has been detached from the claim it constrains.

Keep a contrast when both halves carry information or it corrects a relevant belief. Keep a list when its items are genuinely distinct. Keep passive voice when the actor is unknown or the genre benefits. Choose punctuation by the relationship between clauses, not by a fixed quota.

## Preservation review

Compare source and revision, not merely the revision against a style checklist.

1. Check changed claim-bearing sentences for stronger certainty, lost conditions, reversed causality or attribution drift.
2. Check quantities with their units, comparison groups, time periods and sample scope. Matching a number alone is insufficient.
3. Check quotations, reference labels, links, formulas, code and identifiers. Keep each citation attached to the claim it supports.
4. Check personal statements against the source or author sample. A stylistic edit cannot supply a new experience or belief.
5. Check the requested length, structure, genre and return format. Remove editorial residue from final copy.

If a factual error is suspected in the source, preserve and flag it outside the revision unless factual correction was authorized. If an important phrase cannot be improved without settling an unresolved meaning, keep it and identify the ambiguity.

A material preservation failure triggers a targeted correction or a return to the source passage. After one ordinary correction pass, report any remaining conflict. Do not hide it behind a smoother sentence.
